# Executive Decision Recorded

## Status

Domain Model v1.0

---

# Purpose

The Executive Decision Recorded Domain Event records the successful commitment of executive leadership to a business decision following completion of a commercial Assessment.

Its purpose is to establish the moment at which trustworthy commercial intelligence becomes organizational intent while preserving executive sovereignty, accountability, and complete business lineage.

Executive Decision Recorded marks the beginning of organizational action.

It does not execute organizational change.

---

# Definition

Executive Decision Recorded is an immutable Domain Event representing the successful recording of an Executive Decision following review of a completed Assessment.

Once published, the Executive Decision becomes part of the permanent commercial history of the Organization.

---

# Why This Event Exists

FLUANZ exists to provide trustworthy commercial intelligence.

Executive leadership exists to determine organizational action.

The Recommendation Service provides guidance.

The Executive Decision represents human judgment.

This event permanently records the moment at which leadership commits to a business direction.

---

# Business Meaning

When this event occurs, the business can truthfully say:

> "Executive leadership has formally committed to a business decision."

The commercial reasoning process has concluded.

Organizational execution may now begin.

---

# Business Preconditions

The following conditions must already be satisfied:

- The Assessment has completed.
- Revenue Readiness has been calculated.
- Recommendations have been generated.
- Executive leadership has reviewed the available commercial intelligence.
- The Executive Decision has been formally recorded.

If any condition fails, this event is never published.

---

# Business Consequences

After this event occurs:

- Executive intent officially exists.
- Organizational execution may begin.
- Commercial accountability is established.
- Future Assessments may evaluate the outcome of this decision.
- Complete decision lineage is preserved.

---

Executive Decision

# Relationship to Domain Services
