# Purpose

The Website Architecture defines the structure, navigation, user flow, and content hierarchy of the SalesFluance Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure every visitor can easily understand our services, navigate the platform, build trust, and convert into a qualified sales opportunity.

This document serves as the source of truth for developers, designers, marketers, and content creators.

---

# Website Goals

The website must achieve five primary objectives.

1. Build Trust

Present SalesFluance as a professional B2B growth partner.

2. Generate Qualified Leads

Encourage visitors to schedule a discovery call.

3. Educate Prospects

Explain services, industries, and business value.

4. Showcase Expertise

Demonstrate knowledge through resources and industry content.

5. Scale

Support future products, industries, and platform features.
