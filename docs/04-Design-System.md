# Purpose

The Design System establishes the visual, interaction, and component standards used throughout the SalesFluance Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure every page, feature, and future product delivers a consistent, premium, enterprise-grade experience.

This document serves as the design reference for developers, designers, and AI-assisted development tools.

---

# Design Principles

Every interface must follow these principles.

1. Outcome Focused

Every section should guide visitors toward a business outcome.

2. Enterprise Trust

Professional, credible, and clean.

3. Simplicity

Remove unnecessary complexity.

4. Consistency

Identical components behave identically.

5. Accessibility

Readable, keyboard-friendly, and inclusive.

6. Performance

Fast loading with smooth interactions.

7. Mobile First

Every experience must work seamlessly on mobile before desktop enhancements.
