# Architecture Decision Records

## Purpose

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) preserve every architectural decision made throughout the evolution of the FLUANZ platform.

Every architectural change must be traceable to:

- Architecture Reviews
- Business discoveries
- Engineering principles
- Constitutional changes

No architectural modification is made without an ADR.

---

## ADR Status

Each ADR shall have one of the following statuses:

- Proposed
- Accepted
- Accepted with Modification
- Rejected
- Superseded

---

## Decision Authority

Architecture decisions are evaluated by the Chief Domain Architect.

Independent reviews inform decisions but do not automatically modify the architecture.
