# DDD Workshop 02

# Revenue Readiness Discovery

Status:
Discovery Workshop

Purpose:
Discover the true business meaning of Revenue Readiness before defining any domain objects, scores, calculations, or implementation models.

This workshop intentionally avoids software design.

The objective is to discover reality, not model software.

---

# Workshop Question

What is Revenue Readiness?

---

# What Revenue Readiness Is NOT

Revenue Readiness is not:

- A dashboard
- A KPI
- A report
- A percentage
- A readiness score
- An AI prediction
- A software feature

These are representations.

Not the phenomenon itself.

---

# Historical Thought Experiment

Imagine FLUANZ exists in 1926.

No computers.

No CRM.

No databases.

No AI.

A team of experienced commercial auditors spends weeks reviewing:

- Sales processes
- Customer records
- Financial reports
- Territory coverage
- Sales correspondence
- Hiring activity
- Market conditions

At the end they present a leather-bound executive report.

Question:

What exactly have they measured?

---

# Candidate Definition

Revenue Readiness is:

"The measurable organizational capability to consistently transform commercial opportunities into predictable revenue."

This is a candidate definition.

It is deliberately treated as a hypothesis until validated.

---

# Revenue Readiness Exists Independent of Software

Revenue Readiness exists whether or not FLUANZ exists.

Software merely observes it.

The platform does not create readiness.

It measures, explains, and improves readiness.

---

# Characteristics

Revenue Readiness is:

- Dynamic
- Contextual
- Multi-dimensional
- Observable
- Explainable
- Actionable
- Continuously evolving

Revenue Readiness is NOT:

- Binary
- Static
- Absolute
- Guaranteed

---

# Key Questions

Does Revenue Readiness exist continuously?

Can readiness improve without a new assessment?

Can readiness deteriorate automatically?

Can two organizations have identical readiness scores while possessing different strengths?

Can readiness ever be complete?

Does readiness have an upper limit?

---

# Invariants

Revenue Readiness cannot exist without an Organization.

Revenue Readiness cannot be directly modified.

Revenue Readiness changes only because organizational reality changes.

Assessments observe readiness.

They do not create readiness.

Recommendations influence readiness.

They do not define readiness.

---

# Relationship to FLUANZ

FLUANZ does not sell assessments.

FLUANZ improves Revenue Readiness.

Assessments are merely one mechanism through which the platform observes and explains readiness.

---
